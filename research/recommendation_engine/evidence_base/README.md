# Evidence Base & Source Traceability (`evidence_base/`)

## Purpose
This directory stores documentation, clinical/educational literature references, and external supervisor/expert feedback that ground the MathQuest recommendation engine in recognized evidence.

## Contents & Scope
1. **Clinical & Practice Guidelines:**
   - **NICE NG87 (National Institute for Health and Care Excellence):** Evidence-based recommendations for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder diagnosis and management (environmental adaptations, structured routines).
   - **AAP 2019 (American Academy of Pediatrics):** Clinical practice guideline for the diagnosis, evaluation, and treatment of ADHD in children and adolescents.
   - **Boston Children's Hospital Guidance:** Pediatric neurodevelopmental learning and executive functioning support strategies.
2. **Mathematics Intervention Standards:**
   - **IES Practice Guide (Institute of Education Sciences):** Assisting Students Struggling with Mathematics: Response to Intervention (RtI) for Elementary and Middle Schools (concrete-representational-abstract sequencing, systematic scaffolding, visual representations).
3. **External Expert Feedback Log:**
   - Structured logs recording feedback, review notes, and adjustments suggested by external clinical psychologists, pediatric experts, and Sri Lankan primary mathematics educators.
4. **Source Traceability Matrix:**
   - Direct mapping linking every recommendation strategy code to its underlying clinical or pedagogical citation.
