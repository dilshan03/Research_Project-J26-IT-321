# Research Evidence Mapping Matrix (`evidence_mapping.md`)

**Component:** Learner Profile & Prediction Engine (SLIIT Final-Year Research J26-IT-321)  
**Purpose:** Maps synthesized clinical and pedagogical evidence from literature sources and expert consultations to the ten core research functions of the engine.

> [!NOTE]
> **Page Numbering Notation:** In the tables below, page references explicitly distinguish printed document page numbers from PDF reader page numbers (e.g., *printed pp. 5–10 / PDF pp. 12–17*). **Exception:** the Boston Children's Hospital guide has no printed page numbers, so its references give PDF page numbers only (PDF p. 1 = cover).
> 
> **Strategy vs. Threshold Distinction:** Where published literature supports a general pedagogical or clinical strategy but does not define an algorithmic software decision threshold, that distinction is explicitly recorded, and the threshold is marked as **Requires expert review**.
