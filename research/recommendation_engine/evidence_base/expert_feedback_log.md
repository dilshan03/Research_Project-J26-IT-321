## 1. Review Context & Purpose

The initial MathQuest rule draft (*MathQuest | Expert Review Pack*, October 2026, printed pp. 1–3 / PDF pp. 1–3) formulated eight preliminary pedagogical and safeguard rules (R1–R8) for review:
1. Examine plausibility and developmental appropriateness for Sri Lankan primary school children (aged 6–10).
2. Establish safeguards against premature, biased, or stigmatizing labeling of children.
3. Strictly separate educational support recommendations from clinical ADHD diagnosis.

## 3. Methodological Integrity & Validation Boundaries

- **Consultation Status:** This document records **user-reported verbal expert consultation** conducted with Ms. Pathmasri Sivalingam (Clinical Psychologist). It does **not** constitute formal written psychometric validation, binding clinical endorsement, or a completed validation record.
- **Protocol Standards:** Formal expert validation under the MathQuest research protocol requires structured item-level judgment rubrics, documented revision logs, and multi-reviewer evaluation (*MathQuest Expert Review Pack*, Section E, printed p. 3 / PDF p. 3).
- **Non-Diagnostic Scope:** No algorithm, heuristic rule, or classification model in MathQuest is authorized to diagnose ADHD, assign clinical presentation labels from gameplay, or claim clinical treatment efficacy based on this preliminary review.
